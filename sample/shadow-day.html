Sampleton Supply Co. · Office of the owner

Shadow Day with Betty Sampleton, office manager
One page, for the owner

Bottom line: three fixes would give Betty back about 9 hours a week, most of it Friday afternoons. All three can be built around the tools she already uses. Nothing here replaces her; it stops her doing work a computer should do.

The two lists

What Betty wants fixed

  • "I copy every order out of email into the order sheet."
  • "The Friday sales report eats my whole afternoon."
  • "Customers call to ask where their order is, and I have to go look."

What we noticed

  • Order totals are typed twice, once in the sheet and once in accounting, and the two disagree about once a week.
  • The Friday report uses the same five numbers every week, pulled by hand from three places.
  • Only Betty knows the steps. If she's out sick, orders wait.

The plan

FixWhat changes for BettyHours back a week
Order intake formCustomers fill in one simple form. Orders land in her sheet on their own, typed once.4
Report that builds itselfThe Friday report fills in from the sheet and accounting. She reviews it instead of building it.3.5
Order status pageCustomers check their own order status. Fewer "where's my order" calls.1.5

Hours are illustrative for this sample. In a real plan they come from what we timed on the Shadow Day.

Price and timing

A fixed price for each fix appears here in a real plan, with no subscription. A working prototype of the order form within a day of saying yes, shown to Betty before anyone else. The full build after she signs off on the prototype.

What we'll check in 30 days

  • Hours Betty spends on orders and the Friday report, against today
  • Order totals matching between the sheet and accounting
  • Betty's goal, in her words: "Leave by 4:30 on Fridays."

Everything Betty showed us stays confidential and belongs to Sampleton Supply Co.
