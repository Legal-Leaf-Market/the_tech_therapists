Every company has a Betty.

She's been there twenty years. She knows where every number comes from and why the report has to be built the way it is. Half your processes live in her head.

She's also tired. The tools she was handed make her copy, paste, retype and double-check, so she stays late, and the work she's best at gets the hours that are left.

Betty isn't the problem. The system she works in is.

Most consultants walk in, sell you a new platform, and make Betty learn it from scratch. We do the opposite. We keep what works for her, fix what doesn't, and nothing is finished until Betty says her job got easier.

How it works

Seven steps, named like therapy, because that's what the listening is.

  1. 1

    Intake

    A free 30-minute call with the owner. What hurts, who carries it, what it costs.

  2. 2

    Shadow Day

    One day beside Betty, with her permission and a signed NDA. We watch and take notes. No pitch.

  3. 3

    Two lists

    Betty tells us what she wants fixed, in her words. We add what we noticed that she's stopped seeing.

  4. 4

    The plan

    One page for the owner: both lists, how we'd fix each, the hours it saves, and a fixed price.

  5. 5

    Prototype

    Something working in about a day, on sample data, shown to Betty first.

  6. 6

    The build

    The real version, shaped around how Betty already works. Done when she says it's easier.

  7. 7

    30-day check-up

    Free. Did the numbers move? And did Betty get back what the old way was costing her?
