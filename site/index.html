How a session works

The therapy language is also the process. Seven steps, one fixed price.

  1. Intake

    A free 30-minute call with the boss. What hurts, who carries it, what it costs.

  2. The session

    We sit with your person for half a day, with permission and an NDA. We watch the real day, record only with consent, and note every "I hate when this happens."

  3. Diagnosis

    A one-page write-up in plain English: the three biggest time drains, hours lost per week, and what each fix would take.

  4. Treatment plan

    A fixed price for the fix. No subscription, no renewal.

  5. The prototype

    Something working in about 24 hours, on sample data, shown to the person who does the job first.

  6. Recovery

    We build the real version around how your team already works. It is done when they sign off.

  7. Check-up

    A free 30-day follow-up call. Anything later is hourly, and only if you want it.

What we fix

  • Spreadsheet sprawl

    Spreadsheets and email chains that should be one simple tool.

  • Double entry

    Retyping the same data into two or three systems.

  • Hand-built reports

    The report the boss wants that takes hours to put together by hand.

  • Confusing forms

    Websites and forms that confuse customers or staff.

  • Field to office

    Paperwork between the job site and the office: claims, inspection photos, job tracking.

  • The big handoff

    When a key person retires, we get what they know out of their head and into a tool.

How we work

  • We treat the process, not the people. The person doing the job is never the problem.
  • Plumber, not car dealership. Patch the leak and keep the bill small.
  • You own it. Code, logins and hosting are in your name. No lock-in.
  • A person signs off. AI does the heavy lifting; one of us checks every piece before you see it.
  • What you tell us stays here. Confidentiality in writing, never shared between clients.
